# MYIO Store Dashboard Plans

## MYIO Essential --- "How much did I consume?"

Target customers: - Small restaurants - Clinics - Pharmacies - Small
grocery stores

Features: - Current month consumption (from day 1 until today) -
Previous closed month consumption - Total energy consumption only -
Total water consumption only - Temperature average, minimum and
maximum - Reservoir average, minimum and maximum - Monthly PDF report -
12 months historical retention - Multi-user access - Mobile access

Limitations: - No alarms - No hourly or daily breakdown - No insights -
No goals - No KPIs

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## MYIO Business --- "What happened and where?"

Includes everything in Essential plus:

Features: - Custom reports up to 90 days - Hourly and daily breakdowns -
KPI dashboard - Up to 5 active alarms - PDF, Excel and CSV exports -
REST API access - Consumption trends - Comparative analysis between
periods

Suggested KPIs: - kWh per square meter - kWh per customer - Liters per
customer - Percentage of temperature compliance - Reservoir autonomy
estimation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## MYIO Enterprise --- "How can I optimize my operation?"

Includes everything in Business plus:

Features: - Up to 20 alarms - AI Insights - Goals and targets -
Performance score - Benchmarking - Equipment ranking - Predictive
maintenance - Digital Twin visualization - Sensor SLA monitoring -
Operational health score

AI Insights examples: - Possible water leakage - HVAC consuming above
baseline - Refrigeration degradation detected - Peak demand optimization
opportunity

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## MYIO Corporate --- "How can I manage my entire network?"

Includes everything in Enterprise plus:

Features: - Multi-site management - Network benchmarking - Executive
dashboards - ESG score - Corporate goals - Store ranking - Cross-site
comparison

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Dashboard Central Layout

The center area is organized into four operational groups:

1.  Energy
2.  Water
3.  Temperature
4.  Reservoirs and Water Tanks

Each group contains: - Summary cards - Device cards - Status
indicators - Trend charts - Quick actions
